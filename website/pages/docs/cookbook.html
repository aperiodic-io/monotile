Bars, costs, flow, risk, screens, data quality and sensors. Every recipe runs in CI over the files below, and its answer must equal DuckDB's for the same files. The tables show the first rows of that answer.

The data: an hour of trades and quotes for BTC, ETH and SOL, a table of instruments, and readings from three devices. Run any recipe on them as written, or on your own files with the same columns.
